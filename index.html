Why the cash flow statement seems daunting

The math takes minutes. The digging takes days.

Your cash flow template picks up the balance changes. Deciding what belongs in operating, investing or financing, and what isn't cash at all, means going back into the GL line by line.

The solution

Build your cash flow statements with full support, without the digging

Sigmaflo does the digging. You make the calls.

Sigmaflo connects to your ERP, traces each transaction to sort it into cash and non-cash, and flags anything that needs your decision. The finished statement links every number to its GL transactions and your decisions, in one audit-ready package.
